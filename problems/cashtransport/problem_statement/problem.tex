\problemname{Cash Transport}
CSN just sent you this months' student loan. Finally you have enough money to rent DBus, the very own van of the computer science student division. You've thought about going on a roadtrip but as the responsible student you are you've decided to also use this possibility to earn money. And is there a better strat in the capitalist grind than robbing armored cash transports?

There are $N$ banks. Bank $i$ has a balance of $\$a_i$ day 0. During the
following $M$ days there will be a cash transport every day. A cash transport
goes from bank $A$ to bank $B$ and takes all of the money in bank $A$ and gives
it to bank $B$. During your time with dBus you will have time to rob $K$ cash
transports. How much money can you rob at most?

\section*{Input}
The first line of input contains three integers $N$, $M$ and $K$ ($1 \le N \le
3 \cdot 10^5$, $1 \le M \le 3 \cdot 10^5$ and $1 \le K \le N$).

\noindent
The second line contains $N$ space-separated integers $a_0, a_1, ..., a_N$ where
$0 \le a_i \le 10^9$ holds.

\noindent
The following $M$ lines contains integers $A$ and $B$ ($0 \le A, B < N$, $A \neq B$) where
$A$ and $B$ are index for between which banks the cash transport occurs.

\section*{Output}
Output a single integer: the maximum amount you can steal from the cash
transports.
