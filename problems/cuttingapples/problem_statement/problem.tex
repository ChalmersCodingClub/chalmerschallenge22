\problemname{Apple Cutting}

\noindent Few things are as associated with Christmas as porridge and
\textit{Julkalendern}. Julia and Loke are up surprisingly early to watch today's
episode being shown in \textit{Focus}, the physics students' gathering place.
They have prepared porridge for themselves and in true Christmas spririt they
have also brought an apple to cut up and split between them.

\noindent The apple is a perfectly spherical ball of radius $R$. Loke is a picky
eater, eating only perfect cubes of side length $S$. Julia, on the contrary,
eats pieces of apple in all shapes and sizes.  They want to share the apple
evenly, so neither can eat more than $S^3$ more volume units of apple than the
other.

\noindent Each cut splits the apple along an arbitrarily oriented half-plane.
Pieces of apple can be translated and rotated between cuts, splitting multiple
pieces of apple in a single cut. How many cuts are necessary so that the
remaining pieces can be shared fairly?

\section*{Input}

The input contains the postive integers $R$ and $S$ ($1 \le R, S \le 10^8$) on a
single line, separated by a space.

\section*{Output}

Output one integer, the least number of cuts required for Julia and Loke to
share the apple. If this is not possible, output "impossible".
