Kakor

Problem ID: kakor

There are $n$ cookies in a line on the table numbered $1$ to $n$ from the left to the right. Cookie number $i$ takes $a_ i$ bites to eat. Gustav and Oskar want to eat the cookies, but they couldn’t agree on how to divide the cookies between themselves, so they decided to play a game. They will take turns playing a move. A move consists of either

  • taking a cookie from the table, or

  • taking a bite from a previously taken cookie

It is not allowed to take another cookie before finishing the last one. When there are no cookies left and one player has finished his last cookie, he has to wait while the other player finishes his.

Oskar’s strategy is to always take the cookie furthest to the left (lowest index) when he can take a new cookie. Gustav makes the first move and wants to eat as many cookies as possible. How many cookies can he eat if he knows about Oskar’s strategy and plays optimally?
